# Demo script (five minutes)

Run `make demo` first (after copying both `.env.example` files), then
open `backend/scripts/demo_login_sheet.local.md` for the accounts below.
Say the bracketed line at each step; click the thing right after it.

## 1. The resident's three original problems (90 seconds)

Log in as the **habit suggestion resident**
(`resident6@harbortower.example.com` on the sheet).

> "A resident waits for the voice assistant, dials every time, and has
> to remember which ticket is theirs."

Point at the home screen's "Bring my car" button.

> "They want to book ahead for a 7 PM event without a phone call."

Open the Schedule tab, show "New," note the ready-by time field.

> "And they leave at the same time every weekday. The app noticed that on
> its own."

Point at the suggestion card at the top of Home: "You usually call your
car around 9:14 AM on Tue, Wed, Fri." Tap **Schedule it** and show it land
on the Schedule tab as a real recurring schedule.

## 2. Live status and the valet side (60 seconds)

Log in as the **resident with an in-flight request**
(`resident1@harbortower.example.com`). Point at the live status card:
Called, with a "Ready by" and "Latest" time.

In a second browser tab, log in as the **valet**
(`valet1@harbortower.example.com`). Show the queue: real tasks, a
countdown to deadline, and the garage lane/depth for each car.

> "Everything on this queue came from a real simulated history and a real
> departure-aware spot assignment, not fixtures."

## 3. The manager board and the what-if panel (90 seconds)

Log in as the **manager** (`manager1@harbortower.example.com`). Show the
state counts (320+ picked-up requests from 30 days of seeded history) and
the on-time rate.

Scroll to "What if?" Move the valet slider from 6 to 10.

> "This runs a real ten-seed simulation of today's scenario, not a lookup
> table, in under a second."

Watch the on-time rate move and the confidence interval update live.

## 4. The policy result and the tamper demonstration (90 seconds)

Open `reports/policies_summary.md` (or the printed table) and point at
the departure-aware assignment line:

> "Across 30 paired seeds, departure-aware spot assignment cut relocations
> per retrieval and raised the on-time rate, both with confidence
> intervals that exclude zero. This is the blocking-relocation problem
> from container terminals, applied to valet parking."

Then open `reports/tamper_demo.txt`:

> "This is a raw SQL edit to a stored audit event, bypassing the
> database's own append-only trigger the way a privileged attacker could.
> The hash chain catches it anyway and names the exact broken link."

Run `make audit` live if there's time to show the same output fresh.

## Closing line

> "Every number I just showed you is sitting in a file in reports/ or
> docs/, generated by a script, not typed by hand. reports/RESULTS.md
> pulls them all together, and the README links each one to its source."
